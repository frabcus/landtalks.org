hook: "Social housing was supposed to loosen the grip of background on destiny."

But local eligibility rules mean a tenant who moves loses their place in the queue, and a tenancy is worth far more in productive London than anywhere else in Britain. Zach quantifies the state-made chokehold, then asks what would have to change for social housing to actually improve social mobility.
