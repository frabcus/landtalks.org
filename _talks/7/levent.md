So why does Britain barely have any? Levent's answer: volunteer management, tiny scale, rents too low to build capital. His fix? Collective Ownership: a co-op of co-ops that pools the professionals, the scale and the money, but leaves residents running their homes. If it works, every renter in Britain will want in.
