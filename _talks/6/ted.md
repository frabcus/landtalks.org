Or is it? The Land Use Framework – four years in the making, two governments in the writing – claims there's enough land for homes, food, nature and solar. Ted introduces it and asks whether a document that cannot direct a single planning decision is a bold new vision, or just Whitehall's latest ruse to change nothing.
