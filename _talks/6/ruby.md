Renting with no security. Buildings designed for the prototypical family. Ruby and her comrades are fed up – so they're setting up a housing co-op. Ruby shares the wins so far, the obstacles they didn't see coming and the plan from here. Spoiler: there's a very long way between a good idea and a new front door.
