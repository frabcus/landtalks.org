Now imagine 300,000 of them – the number we need per year – and suddenly the question of where they'll fit becomes impossible to ignore. Euan takes us on a visual journey through different housing types, from sprawling suburbs to compact terraces, and asks us to re-imagine the sorts of towns and cities we want to build.
