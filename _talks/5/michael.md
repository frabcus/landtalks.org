People resist development because they've got every reason to. The government's reforms – lowering standards, mandating densities, overriding local plans – risk deepening that problem, not solving it. If he's right, stripping away safeguards doesn't build homes; it builds barricades. Michael reframes the debate and teases a different starting point.
