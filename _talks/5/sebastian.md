---
title: "Permit to Thrill: What if a Café Outbid a Range Rover?"
speaker: "Sebastian Vetterlein"
evening: 5
emoji: 🅿️
hook: "Cities weren't built for cars – they just moved in, never paying fair rent."
---

Sebastian traces how cars colonised our streets, explores how we sleepwalked into subsidising their storage on prime public land, and asks a provocative question: what if every parking space went to auction? Could a restaurant's terrace, a florist or even a pocket garden outbid parking for space?
